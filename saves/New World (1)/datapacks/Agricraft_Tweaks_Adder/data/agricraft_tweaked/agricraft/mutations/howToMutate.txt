Hi there!

This text file is to explain how mutations work in this datapack!

Lets say we have a plant, plant1.

How can it breed with other plants?
Well, if plant1 is a main plant for a level, then breeding with itself can get other plants up to one above its level.
Otherwise, the highest it can breed on its own is its own level.

There are 4 levels:
    --cot (special)
    --order
    --set
    --tribe

If a plant is the only plant at said level, it can breed with other plants one level above to handle stuff like that.
At the order level, however you can only breed plants from the same cot:
There are three cots:
    --Monocots
    --Euclidcots
    --None/Special

Here are the chances of breeding plant1 from other crops:
    50% nothing happens
    25% comes from tribe members and the main plant of the tribe
    15% comes from set members and main plant of the set
    10% comes from order members and the main plant of the order

Here are the chances if plant1 is the main tribe member and you don't have any other members of that tribe
    75% nothing happens
    15% comes from set members and the main plant of the set
    10% comes from order members and the main plant of the order

Here are the chances if plant1 is the main set member and you don't have any other members of that set
    90% nothing happens
    10% comes from order members and the main plant of the order

Here are the chances if plant1 is the main order member and you dont have any other members of that order
    5% comes from other main plant members of other orders

There are of course some exceptions:
    Spiceleaves have a 20% chance of occuring from tulip breeding
    Tealeaves have a 20% chance of occuring from Spiceleaves
    Candleberries are twice as rare
    Elderberries are 4x as rare without candleberries and 2x as common with candleberries
    Sweet Berries require spiceleaves and candleberries/elderberries, with elderberries giving it an easier time to appear

    TREES ARE SPECIAL and juniper berries are in the tree section despite your objection, requiring sweet berries to appear.
